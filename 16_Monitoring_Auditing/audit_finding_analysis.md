# Audit Finding Analysis — Access Review Evidence Gap

## Finding Description
Quarterly access reviews were required according to policy. Evidence was available for only 2 out of 4 required review periods.

## Finding Type
Control effectiveness gap caused by incomplete evidence for access review control execution.

## Risk Assessment
**Risk Level:** High

Lack of evidence means the organization cannot demonstrate that access controls were operating effectively during the missing periods.  
This creates:
- Unauthorized access risk
- Regulatory non-compliance risk
- Audit failure risk
- Reduced accountability and traceability

From an audit perspective, absence of evidence is treated as absence of control operation.

## Impacted Area
- Access control governance
- User access management
- Audit readiness
