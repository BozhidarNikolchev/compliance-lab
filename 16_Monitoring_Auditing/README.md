# Topic 20 — Monitoring, Auditing & Continuous Compliance

This topic covers how compliance controls are monitored, audited, and continuously validated in real organizations.  
The focus is on operational compliance, evidence handling, audit findings, and corrective/preventive actions (CAPA).

## Key Areas
- Compliance monitoring vs compliance auditing
- Internal vs external audits
- Audit evidence requirements
- Audit findings and risk severity
- Corrective and preventive actions
- Continuous compliance concepts

## Outcome
After completing this topic, I can:
- Support internal and external audits
- Assess control effectiveness based on evidence
- Document audit findings professionally
- Propose corrective and preventive actions
- Communicate compliance risks clearly
