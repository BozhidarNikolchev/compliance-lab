# Job Application Strategy

## Primary Objective

Secure an entry-level compliance, GRC, risk, or audit support position.

## Approach

- Apply consistently
- Continue portfolio refinement
- Practice interviews regularly
- Tailor applications to role requirements

## Focus Areas

- International companies
- Remote opportunities
- Compliance programs
- Governance and risk functions

## Success Principle

Apply while improving.

Do not wait for perfection before applying.
