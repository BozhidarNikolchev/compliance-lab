### Compliance Analyst

Responsibilities:
- Monitoring
- Audits
- Evidence management
- Reporting
- Remediation tracking

### Junior GRC Analyst

Responsibilities:
- Risk management
- Governance support
- Control monitoring
- Findings management

### Risk & Compliance Analyst

Responsibilities:
- Compliance reporting
- Risk tracking
- KPI and KRI monitoring

### Audit Support Analyst

Responsibilities:
- Audit preparation
- Evidence collection
- Audit coordination
