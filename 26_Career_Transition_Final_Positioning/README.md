# Topic 30 — Career Transition & Final Positioning

This topic focuses on transitioning from compliance training into professional employment.

The emphasis is on job readiness, interview positioning, portfolio utilization, and application strategy.

## Key Areas

- Career readiness assessment
- Role targeting
- Professional positioning
- Interview strategy
- Portfolio presentation
- Job application planning

## Outcome

After completing this topic, I can:

- Position myself professionally for compliance roles
- Explain my portfolio confidently
- Target appropriate entry-level roles
- Communicate my strengths effectively
- Build a structured job search strategy
