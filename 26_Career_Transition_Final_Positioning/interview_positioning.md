# Interview Positioning

## Professional Introduction

I am a junior compliance professional with structured training and portfolio-based experience in compliance operations, risk management, auditing, governance, and remediation processes.

## Positioning Strategy

Do not present yourself as an expert.

Instead:

Present yourself as a highly prepared junior professional with strong analytical skills and a documented compliance portfolio.

## Key Message

I understand how compliance programs operate, how controls are evaluated, how findings are managed, and how remediation activities are tracked through closure.
