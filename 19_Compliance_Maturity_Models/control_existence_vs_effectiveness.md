# Control Existence vs Control Effectiveness

## Control Existence
Refers to whether a control is formally documented and implemented.
Examples:
- Written policy
- Defined procedure
- Assigned responsibility

## Control Effectiveness
Refers to whether the control operates reliably and can be proven through evidence.
Examples:
- Logs demonstrating execution
- Review records
- Consistent results over time

## Key Compliance Insight
Auditors and regulators focus primarily on control effectiveness.
Controls that exist but cannot be proven to operate effectively are treated as failed controls.

Low-maturity programs emphasize existence.
Higher-maturity programs emphasize effectiveness.
