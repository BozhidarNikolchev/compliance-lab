# Regulatory Perspective on Compliance Maturity

## Regulatory Expectations
Regulators understand that gaps exist.
Their focus is on whether the organization:
- Knows its risks
- Owns its weaknesses
- Tracks issues transparently
- Improves over time

## Maturity vs Perfection
Organizations with honest, medium maturity and continuous improvement often receive better regulatory outcomes than organizations claiming high maturity without evidence.

## Key Insight
Regulators reward:
- Credibility
- Accountability
- Progress

They penalize:
- Repeated failures
- Weak ownership
- Defensive or misleading behavior
