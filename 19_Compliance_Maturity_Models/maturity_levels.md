# Compliance Maturity Levels

## Level 1 — Ad Hoc / Initial
- Compliance activities are reactive
- Heavy reliance on individuals
- Limited documentation
- Weak ownership and accountability

Risk:
- High likelihood of repeated findings
- Poor audit and inspection outcomes

## Level 2 — Defined
- Policies and procedures are documented
- Roles and responsibilities defined
- Basic controls implemented

Limitations:
- Controls are inconsistently applied
- Limited monitoring and testing

## Level 3 — Managed
- Controls are monitored and tested
- Issues are logged and tracked
- Evidence is structured and retrievable
- Audit and inspection processes supported

This is a common target maturity level for many organizations.

## Level 4 — Optimized
- Continuous monitoring in place
- Risk-based prioritization
- Metrics and KPIs used effectively
- Culture of continuous improvement

Regulators view this level as strong governance maturity.
