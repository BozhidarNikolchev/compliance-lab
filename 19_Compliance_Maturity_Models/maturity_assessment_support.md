# Compliance Analyst Role in Maturity Assessments

## Responsibilities
Compliance analysts typically:
- Support control mapping to maturity levels
- Collect and validate evidence of effectiveness
- Identify recurring issues
- Document maturity gaps
- Track improvement actions

## Analyst Value
While analysts do not usually design maturity models, their work enables:
- Accurate assessments
- Credible conclusions
- Evidence-backed improvement plans

This makes analyst-level contributions critical to program-level compliance decisions.
