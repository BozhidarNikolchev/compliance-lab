# Lessons for Compliance Programs

## Core Compliance Insight
Compliance maturity is not measured by the absence of issues, but by how effectively issues are identified, managed, and resolved.

## Characteristics of Strong Programs
- Honest risk classification
- Clear control ownership
- Timely remediation
- Strong evidence management
- Continuous monitoring
- Management involvement

## Characteristics of Weak Programs
- Issue minimization
- Delayed remediation
- Poor documentation
- Defensive behavior toward regulators
- Repeated failures

## Practical Takeaway
Regulators tolerate gaps.
They do not tolerate lack of control, transparency, or accountability.
