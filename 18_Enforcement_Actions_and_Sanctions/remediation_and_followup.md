# Remediation and Regulatory Follow-Up

## Remediation Expectations
Following enforcement actions, regulators typically expect:
- Formal remediation plans
- Clear ownership of actions
- Defined timelines and milestones
- Objective evidence of completion

## Compliance Responsibilities
Compliance functions support remediation by:
- Maintaining issue and action registers
- Tracking deadlines and progress
- Collecting and validating evidence
- Supporting follow-up communications
- Preparing for re-inspections or verification reviews

## Follow-Up Risk
Failure to meet remediation commitments often results in:
- Higher fines
- Expanded enforcement scope
- Increased supervisory scrutiny
