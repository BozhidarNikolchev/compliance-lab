# Enforcement Risk Analysis

## Enforcement vs Audit Findings

| Audit Findings | Enforcement Actions |
|---------------|--------------------|
| Improvement-focused | Consequence-focused |
| Flexible timelines | Mandatory deadlines |
| Internal or third-party | Regulator-issued |
| Limited exposure | Legal and reputational impact |

## Risk Factors Increasing Enforcement Likelihood
- Weak compliance culture
- Reactive instead of proactive controls
- Incomplete or unreliable evidence
- Lack of senior management engagement
- Repeated failure patterns

## Compliance Value
Effective compliance reduces enforcement risk by:
- Identifying issues early
- Tracking remediation transparently
- Demonstrating accountability and control
