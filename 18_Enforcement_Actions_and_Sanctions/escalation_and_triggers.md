# Regulatory Escalation and Enforcement Triggers

## Common Escalation Triggers
Enforcement is usually triggered by patterns rather than isolated incidents.

Typical triggers include:
- Repeated regulatory or audit findings
- Missed or ignored remediation commitments
- Weak control ownership and accountability
- Poor evidence integrity or missing documentation
- Misleading or inconsistent regulator responses
- Lack of management awareness or involvement

## Key Insight
In many cases, the original compliance gap is manageable.
Escalation often occurs due to how the organization responds to identified issues.

## Compliance Function Role
- Identify escalation risk early
- Ensure transparent issue tracking
- Escalate unresolved risks internally
- Maintain accurate and consistent documentation
