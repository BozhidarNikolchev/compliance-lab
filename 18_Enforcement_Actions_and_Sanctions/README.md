# Topic 22 — Enforcement Actions & Sanctions

This topic explains how regulatory findings escalate into enforcement actions and sanctions, and how compliance functions prevent or manage such outcomes.

The focus is on understanding regulator escalation logic, enforcement mechanisms, remediation expectations, and the compliance role during enforcement scenarios.

## Key Areas
- Types of enforcement actions
- Regulatory escalation triggers
- Remediation and follow-up requirements
- Compliance responsibilities during enforcement
- Enforcement prevention through governance and monitoring

## Outcome
After completing this topic, I can:
- Explain how regulatory enforcement works
- Identify early enforcement risk signals
- Support remediation tracking and reporting
- Assist with enforcement-related documentation
- Frame compliance as a preventive risk function
