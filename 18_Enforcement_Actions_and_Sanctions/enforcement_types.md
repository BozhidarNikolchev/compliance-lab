# Types of Enforcement Actions

## Administrative Fines
Monetary penalties imposed by regulators due to confirmed non-compliance.
Fines often scale based on:
- Severity of violation
- Duration of non-compliance
- Organizational size or turnover
- Prior compliance history

## Enforcement Notices and Orders
Formal directives requiring organizations to:
- Stop or restrict activities
- Implement specific controls
- Change governance or processes
- Submit to enhanced supervision

## Mandatory Remediation Plans
Organizations may be required to:
- Propose remediation actions
- Meet fixed deadlines
- Provide evidence of completion
- Accept follow-up inspections

Failure to remediate effectively often results in additional sanctions.

## Public Disclosure
Some enforcement actions are made public, leading to reputational damage through:
- Regulator publications
- Press releases
- Media coverage

## License Restrictions or Revocation
In regulated industries, enforcement may include:
- Activity restrictions
- Suspension of licenses
- Revocation of authorization to operate
