# Audit and Finding

## Audit Objective

Evaluate whether privileged-access reviews were performed effectively throughout the review period.

## Evidence Requested

- Access review reports
- Approval records
- User access inventories

## Audit Result

Evidence was available for only two of four quarters.

Required reviews were not consistently performed.

## Finding Classification

Major Finding

## Rationale

- Sensitive access involved
- Evidence incomplete
- Increased exposure to unauthorized access risk
- Reduced ability to demonstrate compliance
