# Compliance Simulation Overview

## Organization Profile

Medium-sized technology company:

- Approximately 500 employees
- Cloud-based infrastructure
- Handles customer information
- Subject to GDPR requirements

## Scenario

The organization performs quarterly privileged-access reviews to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to sensitive customer information.

An internal audit is scheduled to evaluate the effectiveness of the access review control.

## Objective

Demonstrate the complete lifecycle of compliance management from risk identification to closure.
