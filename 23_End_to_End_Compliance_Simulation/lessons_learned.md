# Lessons Learned

## What Worked

- Internal audit identified the issue
- Compliance escalation process functioned effectively
- Remediation actions were completed on time

## What Failed

- Monitoring lacked escalation capability
- Ownership responsibilities were unclear
- Reporting did not provide adequate visibility

## Improvement Opportunities

- Strengthen governance structure
- Improve monitoring controls
- Expand compliance reporting
- Clarify accountability

## Key Insight

Control existence alone does not ensure compliance.

Control effectiveness, monitoring, and accountability are equally important.
