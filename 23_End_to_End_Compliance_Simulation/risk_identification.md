# Risk Identification

## Risk Statement

Unauthorized access to sensitive customer information.

## Potential Impacts

- Data exposure
- GDPR violations
- Regulatory investigations
- Financial penalties
- Reputational harm

## Risk Assessment

Likelihood:
Medium

Impact:
High

Risk Rating:
High

## Risk Owner

Identity and Access Management Manager

## Risk Treatment Strategy

Implement privileged-access review controls to reduce the likelihood of inappropriate access.
