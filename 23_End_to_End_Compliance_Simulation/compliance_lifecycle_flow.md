# Compliance Lifecycle Flow

## Lifecycle Overview

Risk Identified
↓
Control Implemented
↓
Control Monitored
↓
Audit Conducted
↓
Finding Issued
↓
Root Cause Analysis
↓
Corrective Actions
↓
Preventive Actions
↓
Verification Review
↓
Finding Closed

## Key Principle

Compliance is a continuous lifecycle rather than a one-time activity.

Strong compliance programs continuously monitor, improve, and validate controls over time.
