# Core GRC Modules

## Risks
- Risk identification and classification
- Risk scoring (likelihood and impact)
- Risk ownership and mitigation tracking

## Controls
- Definition of controls linked to risks
- Control ownership and frequency
- Tracking control effectiveness

## Issues / Findings
- Logging audit and compliance findings
- Severity classification
- Root cause documentation
- Status tracking

## Audits & Assessments
- Audit planning and scope definition
- Evidence collection
- Control testing
- Audit reporting

## Remediation
- Corrective and preventive actions
- Assignment of responsibilities
- Deadline tracking
- Evidence of completion
