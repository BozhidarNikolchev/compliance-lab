# Topic 25 — Compliance Tooling & GRC Platforms

This topic focuses on how compliance programs are managed using Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) tools.

The emphasis is on understanding how risks, controls, audits, issues, and remediation activities are structured within centralized systems.

## Key Areas
- GRC platform concepts
- Core modules (risks, controls, issues, audits)
- End-to-end compliance workflows
- Role of compliance analysts in GRC systems
- Limitations and risks of compliance tooling

## Outcome
After completing this topic, I can:
- Explain how GRC tools support compliance operations
- Describe core GRC workflows
- Connect risks, controls, and remediation actions
- Support compliance activities within structured platforms
- Understand the limitations of compliance automation
