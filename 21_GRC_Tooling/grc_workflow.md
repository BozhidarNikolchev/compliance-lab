# GRC Workflow — End-to-End Compliance Lifecycle

## Step 1: Risk Identification
A risk is identified and logged in the system.

## Step 2: Control Mapping
A control is assigned to mitigate the identified risk.

## Step 3: Control Execution & Monitoring
Control activities are performed and monitored regularly.

## Step 4: Audit / Assessment
Controls are tested through audits or reviews.

## Step 5: Finding Identification
If a control fails or is ineffective, a finding is recorded.

## Step 6: Issue Logging
The finding is logged as an issue with severity and ownership.

## Step 7: Remediation Planning
Corrective and preventive actions are defined.

## Step 8: Evidence Collection
Proof of remediation is uploaded and validated.

## Step 9: Issue Closure
The issue is formally reviewed and closed.

## Key Insight
This workflow represents how compliance operates systematically within GRC platforms.
