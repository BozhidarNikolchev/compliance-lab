# Role of a Compliance Analyst in GRC Systems

## Responsibilities
- Maintain accurate records of risks, controls, and issues
- Upload and validate compliance evidence
- Track remediation actions and deadlines
- Support audit processes within the system
- Generate reports and support dashboards
- Ensure data quality and consistency

## Analyst Value
Compliance analysts ensure that the GRC system reflects reality.
Accurate data and consistent updates are critical for effective compliance management.

## Key Mindset
The tool is only as effective as the quality of the data and the discipline of the processes behind it.
