# Root Cause Analysis

## Objective

Determine why required privileged-access reviews were not consistently completed.

## Root Cause 1

Ownership responsibilities were not clearly defined.

## Root Cause 2

No automated reminders or monitoring mechanism existed.

## Root Cause 3

Management reporting did not include access-review performance metrics.

## Conclusion

Control design was adequate.

Control execution, monitoring, and governance oversight were insufficient.
