# Final Conclusion

## Assessment Outcome

The access management control framework is formally established but operational effectiveness is inconsistent.

While control design is adequate, weaknesses in execution, monitoring, and oversight resulted in a major finding.

## Key Recommendations

- Clarify ownership responsibilities
- Improve monitoring capabilities
- Strengthen reporting to management
- Enhance evidence management practices
- Validate control effectiveness on a recurring basis

## Final Statement

Compliance effectiveness depends not only on the existence of controls, but on consistent execution, monitoring, evidence retention, and accountability.
