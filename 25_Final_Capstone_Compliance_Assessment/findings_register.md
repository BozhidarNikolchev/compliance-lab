# Findings Register

## Finding ID

CAP-001

## Finding Title

Inconsistent Execution of Quarterly Access Reviews

## Finding Description

Required quarterly privileged-access reviews were not consistently completed or documented.

Evidence was available for only two quarters during the assessment period.

## Severity

Major Finding

## Business Impact

The organization cannot reliably demonstrate the effectiveness of access governance controls.

This increases compliance and security risk exposure.

## Status

Open
