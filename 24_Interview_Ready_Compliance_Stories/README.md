# Topic 28 — Interview-Ready Compliance Stories

This topic focuses on communicating compliance knowledge effectively during interviews.

The emphasis is on structured answers, practical reasoning, and professional communication using the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) methodology.

## Key Areas

- STAR interview methodology
- Compliance interview preparation
- Audit and evidence scenarios
- Control failure responses
- Regulatory inspection support
- Root cause analysis communication
- Escalation decision-making

## Outcome

After completing this topic, I can:

- Answer compliance interview questions confidently
- Structure responses using the STAR method
- Explain compliance processes clearly
- Discuss audits, risks, and remediation professionally
- Communicate compliance reasoning effectively
