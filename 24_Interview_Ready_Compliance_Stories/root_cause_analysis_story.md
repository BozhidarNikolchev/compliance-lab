# Interview Scenario — Root Cause Analysis

## Example Question

How do you approach compliance failures?

## Approach

1. Establish the facts
2. Collect and validate evidence
3. Identify the affected requirement
4. Identify the affected control
5. Determine root causes
6. Assess organizational impact
7. Develop corrective actions
8. Develop preventive actions
9. Verify effectiveness

## Key Insight

The objective is not to assign blame but to understand causes and prevent recurrence.
