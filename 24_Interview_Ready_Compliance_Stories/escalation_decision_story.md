# Interview Scenario — Escalation Decision

## Example Question

When should a compliance issue be escalated?

## Escalation Criteria

Issues should be escalated when:

- Regulatory obligations may be affected
- Deadlines are missed
- Risk thresholds are exceeded
- Ownership is unclear
- Remediation activities are delayed
- Senior management awareness is required

## Goal

Provide visibility before a manageable issue becomes a significant compliance problem.
