# Compliance Interview Framework

## Requirement

What regulation, policy, or control applies?

## Evidence

What evidence demonstrates compliance?

## Control

What control manages the risk?

## Risk

What happens if the control fails?

## Root Cause

Why did the issue occur?

## Remediation

How will the issue be corrected?

## Prevention

How will recurrence be prevented?

## Verification

How will effectiveness be confirmed?

## Key Insight

This framework can be applied to many compliance interview questions.
