# Interview Scenario — Regulatory Inspection Preparation

## Example Question

How would you support a regulatory inspection?

## Situation

A regulator requested documentation related to compliance controls and governance activities.

## Task

Coordinate evidence collection and ensure response accuracy.

## Action

- Reviewed regulatory request scope
- Collected supporting documentation
- Validated completeness and quality
- Coordinated with process owners
- Maintained version control
- Tracked deadlines

## Result

Requested information was provided accurately and within required timelines.

## Key Competencies Demonstrated

- Regulatory readiness
- Documentation management
- Coordination
- Quality assurance
