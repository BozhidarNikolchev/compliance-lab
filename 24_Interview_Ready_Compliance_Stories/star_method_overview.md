# STAR Method Overview

## Purpose

The STAR framework provides a structured approach for answering behavioral and scenario-based interview questions.

## Situation

Describe the scenario or context.

Example:

A quarterly access review process was found to have missing evidence during an internal audit.

## Task

Explain your responsibility.

Example:

Determine the cause, assess the risk, and support remediation efforts.

## Action

Describe the steps taken.

Example:

- Reviewed available evidence
- Confirmed control ownership
- Investigated root causes
- Documented findings
- Supported corrective actions

## Result

Explain the outcome.

Example:

Control ownership was clarified, monitoring improved, and future reviews were completed successfully.

## Key Insight

Structured answers demonstrate professionalism and analytical thinking.
