# Interview Scenario — Missing Audit Evidence

## Example Question

What would you do if evidence required for an audit could not be located?

## Situation

Evidence supporting a required control could not be located for part of the review period.

## Task

Determine whether the control failed or whether evidence management failed.

## Action

- Reviewed approved evidence repositories
- Confirmed control ownership
- Examined supporting logs and records
- Determined whether control execution occurred
- Documented findings
- Escalated according to procedures

## Result

The root cause was identified, evidence management improved, and additional retention controls were introduced.

## Key Competencies Demonstrated

- Evidence validation
- Audit support
- Root cause analysis
- Escalation management
