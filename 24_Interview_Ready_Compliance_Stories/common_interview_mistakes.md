# Common Compliance Interview Mistakes

## Unstructured Answers

Providing answers without a logical framework makes assessment difficult.

## Jumping to Conclusions

Recommendations should follow fact gathering and analysis.

## Focusing on Blame

Compliance focuses on processes, controls, evidence, and accountability.

## Overstating Experience

Credibility is strengthened by honesty and transparency.

## Ignoring Root Causes

Effective compliance professionals identify underlying causes, not just symptoms.
