# Regulatory Examination Lifecycle

## 1. Pre-Notification Phase
- Formal examination notice received
- Scope, timeframe, and regulatory basis defined
- Initial information request (RFI) issued

Compliance responsibilities:
- Clarify scope and expectations
- Assign internal owners
- Coordinate evidence collection
- Control communication channels

## 2. Evidence & Documentation Review
Regulators review:
- Policies and procedures
- Logs and records
- Training documentation
- Audit results and risk assessments

Key risk:
Mismatch between documented policies and actual practices.

## 3. Interviews & Walkthroughs
Regulators interview compliance staff, management, and process owners to assess:
- Awareness
- Consistency
- Ownership
- Governance culture

## 4. Findings & Observations
Results may include:
- Observations
- Required improvements
- Formal violations

Severity depends on impact, duration, and prior compliance history.

## 5. Remediation & Follow-Up
Organizations must:
- Acknowledge findings
- Propose remediation plans
- Implement corrective actions
- Provide follow-up evidence to regulators
