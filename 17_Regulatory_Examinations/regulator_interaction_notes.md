# Regulator Interaction Notes

## Regulator Expectations
Regulators prioritize:
- Clear control ownership
- Honest risk assessment
- Evidence integrity
- Management awareness
- Timely responses

They are less interested in perfect documentation and more focused on credibility and accountability.

## Best Practices During Inspections
- Provide accurate, scoped responses
- Avoid speculation
- Escalate inconsistencies internally
- Maintain document version control
- Keep communication factual and calm

## Common Pitfalls
- Over-sharing irrelevant documents
- Inconsistent explanations between teams
- Missing response deadlines
- Poor-quality or incomplete evidence

Transparency and structure reduce escalation risk.
