# Topic 21 — Regulatory Examinations & Inspections

This topic focuses on how regulatory authorities conduct examinations and inspections, and how organizations prepare for, respond to, and remediate regulatory findings.

The emphasis is on regulator expectations, evidence quality, internal coordination, and clear accountability.

## Key Areas
- Regulatory examination lifecycle
- Inspection triggers and scope definition
- Evidence and documentation review
- Regulator interviews and walkthroughs
- Findings, remediation, and follow-up

## Outcome
After completing this topic, I can:
- Support regulatory inspection preparation
- Coordinate evidence requests
- Assist during regulator interviews
- Track findings and remediation actions
- Maintain inspection readiness
