# Case Study — Privileged Access Review Failure

## Scenario

Policy requires quarterly privileged-access reviews.

Audit results revealed:

- Only one review completed during the year
- Evidence missing for three quarters
- Former employees retained access

---

## Requirement Violated

- Internal access management policy
- Control monitoring requirements
- Evidence retention requirements

---

## Failed Control

Quarterly privileged access review process.

Classification:
Ineffective Control

The control existed but was not consistently performed.

---

## Root Causes

Potential causes:

- Weak ownership
- Lack of deadline tracking
- Insufficient monitoring
- Inadequate management oversight

---

## Corrective Actions

- Assign formal control ownership
- Perform overdue reviews
- Remove unnecessary access
- Collect remaining evidence where possible

---

## Preventive Actions

- Implement monitoring reminders
- Introduce dashboard reporting
- Escalate missed reviews
- Test control effectiveness regularly

---

## Lessons Learned

Policies alone do not ensure compliance.

Continuous execution, monitoring, and accountability are required.
