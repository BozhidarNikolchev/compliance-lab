# Lessons Learned Process

## Purpose

Capture organizational improvements following compliance failures.

---

## What Worked?

Document controls or processes that functioned correctly.

Example:

Annual audit successfully detected the issue.

---

## What Failed?

Identify weaknesses.

Example:

Monitoring processes failed to identify missed reviews.

---

## What Improvements Are Required?

Examples:

- Enhanced monitoring
- Better reporting
- Additional training
- Control redesign

---

## Who Owns the Improvement?

Every action must have an owner.

Ownership should include:

- Role
- Responsibility
- Due date
- Success criteria

---

## Key Insight

Improvements without ownership rarely become reality.
