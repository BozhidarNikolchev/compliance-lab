# Control Failure Types

## Missing Control

A required control does not exist.

Example:
No user access review process has been implemented.

Risk:
The risk is unmanaged from the start.

---

## Ineffective Control

A control exists but is not operating effectively.

Example:
Access reviews are required but not consistently performed.

Risk:
False confidence and increased audit exposure.

---

## Bypassed Control

A control exists and functions correctly, but individuals intentionally circumvent it.

Example:
Required approval processes are skipped.

Risk:
Governance breakdown and accountability concerns.

---

## Key Insight

The existence of a control does not guarantee effective risk management.

Control effectiveness must be continuously demonstrated through evidence.
