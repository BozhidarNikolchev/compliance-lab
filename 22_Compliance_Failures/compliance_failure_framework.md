# Compliance Failure Analysis Framework

## Objective

Provide a structured approach for analyzing compliance failures.

## Step 1 – What Happened?

Describe the event objectively.

Focus on:
- Facts
- Timeline
- Impact

Avoid:
- Assumptions
- Blame
- Unsupported conclusions

---

## Step 2 – What Requirement Was Violated?

Identify the applicable requirement:

- Regulatory requirement
- Internal policy
- Industry standard
- Contractual obligation

Examples:
- GDPR requirements
- ISO 27001 controls
- Internal access management policy

---

## Step 3 – What Control Failed?

Identify the failed control and classify the failure.

Common categories:
- Missing control
- Ineffective control
- Bypassed control

---

## Step 4 – What Was the Root Cause?

Determine why the failure occurred.

Examples:
- Weak ownership
- Poor monitoring
- Lack of training
- Management oversight failures
- System configuration problems

---

## Step 5 – How Could It Have Been Prevented?

Identify preventive improvements.

Examples:
- Improved monitoring
- Clear ownership
- Automated controls
- Escalation procedures
- Additional training
