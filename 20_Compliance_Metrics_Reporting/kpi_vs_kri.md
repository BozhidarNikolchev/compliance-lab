# KPI vs KRI in Compliance

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
KPIs measure how effectively compliance processes are performing.

Examples:
- Percentage of completed access reviews
- Audit completion rate
- Training completion percentage

KPIs answer:
"Are compliance activities being performed as expected?"

---

## Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
KRIs measure potential or emerging compliance risks.

Examples:
- Number of overdue remediation actions
- Percentage of privileged accounts without review
- Repeat audit findings

KRIs answer:
"Is risk increasing or decreasing?"

---

## Key Insight
KPIs measure performance.
KRIs measure exposure to risk.

Strong compliance programs monitor both.
