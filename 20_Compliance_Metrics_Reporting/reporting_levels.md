# Compliance Reporting Levels

## Operational Reporting
- Detailed and granular metrics
- Used by compliance teams
- Supports day-to-day monitoring

## Management Reporting
- Focus on trends and risks
- Summarized insights
- Supports decision-making

## Board-Level Reporting
- High-level overview
- Focus on major risks and exposures
- Minimal operational detail

---

## Key Insight
Different audiences require different levels of detail and interpretation.
