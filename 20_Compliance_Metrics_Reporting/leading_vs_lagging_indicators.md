# Leading vs Lagging Indicators

## Lagging Indicators
Measure outcomes that have already occurred.

Examples:
- Number of compliance incidents
- Audit findings identified
- Regulatory violations

Limitation:
Lagging indicators are reactive and identify problems after they occur.

---

## Leading Indicators
Measure early warning signals before problems materialize.

Examples:
- Delayed control execution
- Increasing backlog of compliance issues
- Drop in training completion rates
- Missed review deadlines

Advantage:
Leading indicators allow organizations to act before risks escalate.

---

## Key Insight
Mature compliance programs prioritize leading indicators to prevent failures rather than react to them.
