# Compliance Analyst Role in Metrics & Reporting

## Responsibilities
- Collect and validate compliance data
- Maintain dashboards and reports
- Track KPIs and KRIs
- Identify unusual trends or deviations
- Escalate emerging risks

## Analyst Value
Compliance analysts translate raw data into meaningful insights that support risk management and decision-making.

## Key Mindset
Numbers alone are not enough.
Understanding what the numbers mean is critical.
