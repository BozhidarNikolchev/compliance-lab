# Topic 24 — Compliance Metrics, KRIs & Reporting

This topic focuses on how compliance programs measure performance, assess risk exposure, and communicate insights to management using structured metrics.

The emphasis is on KPI vs KRI differentiation, leading and lagging indicators, and how compliance data supports decision-making.

## Key Areas
- Compliance metrics and measurement
- KPI vs KRI distinction
- Leading vs lagging indicators
- Compliance dashboards and reporting
- Data-driven compliance management

## Outcome
After completing this topic, I can:
- Define and distinguish KPIs and KRIs
- Identify meaningful compliance metrics
- Support compliance dashboard reporting
- Detect early compliance risk signals
- Communicate compliance performance using data
